Lo strumento di pubblicazione

I contenuti vengono montati e pubblicati da uno strumento privato che gira sul computer del titolare. Non ha altri utenti: nessuna iscrizione, nessun archivio di utenti, nessun accesso da parte di terzi. L'unico account che tocca è quello del titolare, che è anche l'unica persona che lo usa.

The publishing tool

The videos on these accounts are produced and published by a private desktop tool run by the account owner. It has no other users — no sign-up, no user database, no third-party access. The only account it ever touches is the owner's own.

How it connects to TikTok

PermissionWhat it is used for
user.info.basic Before every post we read the account's current posting constraints (maximum duration, allowed privacy levels, whether comment, duet or stitch are disabled) and build the posting options from that response, instead of from hardcoded values.
video.publish Publishes the finished video directly to the owner's own account, at the time set by the editorial calendar. Until the app review is approved, every post is created with privacy_level=SELF_ONLY.

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok settings; the stored tokens then stop working and are deleted. Details in the privacy policy.
